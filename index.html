The problem

Most learning projects need three vendors. Yours shouldn't.

A typical learning initiative gets split up. A consultant works out what's needed. An eLearning shop builds the courses. A dev agency builds the app or platform. Every hand-off loses context, adds weeks, and leaves nobody owning the question that matters: did people actually learn?

We cover the whole arc with one team. The people who diagnose the problem are the people who build the solution and check whether it worked.
